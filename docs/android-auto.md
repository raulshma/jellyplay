# Android Auto

JellyPlay is an Android Auto **media app**: browse your Jellyfin music
library and control playback from the car's head unit — no car-specific
setup, no extra app. Works with both Android Auto (phone-projected) and
Android Automotive OS (built into the vehicle).

## Requirements

- A phone running Android Auto with JellyPlay installed and **logged in to
  your Jellyfin server at least once** (the car browses the server session
  saved on the phone).
- The phone connected to the head unit (USB or wireless).
- JellyPlay appears in Auto's media app list — tap it to launch.

## What you get

- **Browse**: Recently Played, Recently Added, Artists, Albums, Genres,
  Playlists, Favorites, and completed audio Downloads (offline downloads
  play without a connection to the server).
- **Playback controls**: play/pause, seek, next/previous from the head unit,
  steering-wheel keys, and voice buttons — the same media session that
  drives the notification and Bluetooth headphones.
- **Voice search**: "Play <song/artist/album> on JellyPlay" via Google
  Assistant or the head unit's search.
- **Queue sync**: anything started from the car shows up in the phone's
  queue screen, and vice versa.

## Tips

- **Offline**: download music in the app first (see
  [Offline downloads](./offline-downloads.md)); the Downloads folder in the
  car works with no server connection.
- **Playback quality** follows the app's streaming-quality setting; adjust
  it in Settings → Playback if you're on a metered connection.
- If the app doesn't appear on the head unit, make sure it's installed on
  the phone (not just Android TV builds) and that Android Auto's app list
  isn't filtering it out.
