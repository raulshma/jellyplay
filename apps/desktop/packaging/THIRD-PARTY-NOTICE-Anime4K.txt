JellyPlay third-party notice: Anime4K
=====================================

This distribution bundles a subset of the Anime4K v4.0.1 GLSL shader pack
(the per-mode mpv chains' stage files), an MIT-licensed work by bloc97 and
contributors, unmodified. The shaders are installed from the application
resources into the user's data directory (`shaders/`) at desktop startup,
because mpv's `glsl-shaders` option requires real file paths.

  Upstream project:   https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K
  Retrieved release:  v4.0.1 (asset `Anime4K_v4.0.zip`)
  License:            MIT (https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K/blob/v4.0.1/LICENSE)
  Copyright:          Copyright (c) bloc97

Bundled files (resources/shaders/anime4k/):
  Anime4K_Clamp_Highlights.glsl
  Anime4K_Restore_CNN_VL.glsl
  Anime4K_Restore_CNN_Soft_VL.glsl
  Anime4K_Upscale_Denoise_CNN_x2_VL.glsl
  Anime4K_Upscale_CNN_x2_VL.glsl
  Anime4K_Upscale_CNN_x2_M.glsl
  Anime4K_AutoDownscalePre_x2.glsl
  Anime4K_AutoDownscalePre_x4.glsl
