First release on F-Droid.

JellyPlay is a client for your own Jellyfin media server: movies and shows (mpv/VLC/ffmpeg engines, styled ASS subtitles), music with synced lyrics, photos, live TV, books and comics, offline downloads, Syncplay co-watching, multi-server support and dynamic color themes.

Compared to the Play/GitHub builds, this build drops Google Cast and the in-app self-updater, bundles its fonts (OFL), and cannot read RAR comic archives (CBZ still works).
